Origin myth

The Night the Sky Opened

Seven years ago, a silent star crossed the sky and fell beyond the city walls. It left no crater—only a field of floating glass fragments that glow when someone tells the truth.

People built an archive around the fragments. Some came to study them. Others came to hide what they knew.
